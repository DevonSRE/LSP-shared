Court payment configuration — prices and payout account

How a court sets what it charges lawyers (price per page and CTC price) and the bank account its share is paid into. This is the Court → Lawyer side and is independent of the Devon → Court subscription billing. Every box shows the endpoint it calls (base path /api/v1).

Court Admin means a Judge, Legal Aide or Registrar. Platform Admin can also call these endpoints, but they always act on the caller's own court.

Things worth knowing

  • Prices are held in kobo, not naira — ₦50.00 is 5000.
  • The payout account can be set once. After that, changing it is rejected. Prices stay editable at any time.
  • There is no live payout or split-payment integration behind this yet; the account is stored as reference data.
  • Both a first save and a later update are written to the court's audit trail.
