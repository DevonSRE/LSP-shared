Subscription checkout — pay once per cycle

A court picks a plan, pays on Paystack, and is confirmed on return. Every box shows the endpoint it calls (base path /api/v1). The backend starts the Paystack transaction itself and never trusts an amount or plan sent back from the browser.

Things worth knowing

  • Verify is safe to call twice. A second call for a reference that's already paid returns the current subscription and changes nothing.
  • A court can pay by card or by bank transfer. A transfer often lands after the court has left the page. There is no webhook: while a transfer is pending, the page should keep calling verify (every few seconds at first, then every 30 seconds), and an hourly reconcile job confirms it even if the court has gone. The invoice's paid_via records which way it was paid.
  • Only a card is saved. A court that pays by transfer has no saved card, so it can't switch on auto-renew and pays each cycle through this flow.
  • Renewing early adds a cycle on top of the existing end date, so no paid time is lost.
  • After a successful Paystack payment, every active Judge, Legal Aide and Registrar of the court is emailed a payment confirmation (plain HTML in the approved platform style, not a Mailjet template). It is sent once, and a sending failure never affects the payment. The sample design is email/subscription-payment-confirmation.html.
  • The receipt (step 9) is covered by the invoices flow, and the court's payment history lists every confirmed payment. That endpoint returns the paid invoice's details as JSON; there is no PDF download yet.
  • Every successful payment quietly stores a reusable card when Paystack provides one. That's what later lets the court switch on auto-renew — see the recurring billing flow.
  • A non-published plan can only be checked out by a court that's already on that exact plan (renewal). See the plans workflow.
