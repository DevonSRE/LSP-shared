Things worth knowing

  • Every button on the page maps to the codes above. Match on data.code, not on the message.
  • Plan cards come from ?view=cards. A card is one plan family; each cycle that is offered carries the plan_id to send to checkout. The plain list without view is unchanged.
  • There is no webhook. While a bank transfer is pending, keep calling verify. It is safe to call repeatedly, and a payment is never applied twice. The server also confirms it within the hour if the court stops.
  • The history shows confirmed payments only, including any an admin recorded for the court ("Manual · recorded by Devon"), dated back to before the court joined JudicAI.
  • The confirmation email goes out for Paystack payments (card, transfer, automatic). A payment an admin records by hand is not announced. It shows no VAT or Paystack-fee rows until those amounts are recorded.
  • Suspended courts can still read everything and see this screen. Paying restores them at once. See dunning and suspension.
