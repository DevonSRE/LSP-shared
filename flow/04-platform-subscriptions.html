Platform subscriptions — monitoring courts, settling invoices

A Platform Admin sees where every court stands with its subscription, opens one court to see what it has paid and every attempt it has made, and settles invoices. Invoices come from two places: automatically, every time a court pays or is charged, and by hand when a Platform Admin bills a court outside its normal cycle. Every box shows the endpoint it calls (base path /api/v1).

Things worth knowing

  • The three subscription views are Platform Admin only and read-only. They change nothing.
  • The list shows every court, including ones that have never subscribed (status NONE). start_date and end_date are the currently paid period; subscribed_since is when the court first subscribed.
  • The court detail lists confirmed payments only. A payment made before this release has no period dates or paid-on time, because they weren't recorded then.
  • An attempt is any checkout or automatic charge, whatever happened. Pending means the court abandoned checkout, or a bank transfer hasn't landed yet. Overdue means an automatic charge failed. Ad-hoc invoices raised by an admin are not attempts.
  • Raising, reminding, marking paid and waiving are Platform Admin only. Listing and receipts are open to court users, scoped to their own court.
  • Marking an invoice paid that belongs to a subscription reactivates and extends that subscription the same way a successful Paystack payment does — the logic isn't duplicated.
  • Reminders only work for a court that has paid at least once, because the recipient is the email captured from its last successful payment. There's no wider lookup of court staff yet.
  • The receipt endpoint returns the invoice details. It doesn't generate a downloadable PDF.
